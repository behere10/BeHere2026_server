title: Camera Charging Guidelines
---


## Charging

First, charge the camera body and start with a full charge.

1. Plug the charging cable into the camera.  

   The charging cable and the camera body are marked with round stickers. When charging, align these marks and plug in the charging cable.  

   When charging begins, the status LED will turn off temporarily. In addition, if the LCD power is on, the LCD will turn OFF.

2. Confirm that the status LED is lit red. 

   If the charging cable orientation is incorrect, the status LED will not turn red.

3. Wait for the status LED to turn yellow.  

   When the status LED turns yellow, it is fully charged.

## Charging Time

It is difficult to state an exact time because charging is controlled based on the remaining battery capacity and battery temperature to ensure battery safety.  

When the battery temperature is around room temperature, charging from a completely empty state to a full charge takes about 3 to 4 hours. However, operating until the battery is completely empty is not recommended. A warning will appear when the remaining battery capacity is estimated to fall below approximately 40%. From around 40%, it should reach a full charge in about 2 hours.

Since the battery is housed inside the camera, the temperature tends to be slightly higher, so charging may not proceed at full speed.
