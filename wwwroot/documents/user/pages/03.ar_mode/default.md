title: AR Mode Operation
---


## Transitioning to AR Mode

1. Unplug the charging cable from the camera.

   In principle, please start with the camera fully charged.

2. Confirm that the status LED is dark green.

   This indicates that the camera has entered power saving mode (not charging).

3. Press the side button for about 1 second.

   After a moment, the LCD will turn on.

4. Confirm that the status is displayed on the LCD screen.

   If left untouched in this state for a certain period of time, the LCD power will turn OFF, returning to the power saving mode state (step 2).

5. Press the side button again for about 1 second.

   After checking whether there is new data to display in AR mode, the camera transitions to AR mode.
   However, at this point, the AR space has not been recognized yet.

   Performing this operation in the exhibition area near where 3D models will be displayed makes subsequent AR space recognition smoother.

6. Have the camera recognize the AR space.

   Holding the camera and looking around the exhibition area will allow it to recognize the AR space and display 3D models. Move slowly during this process.


## Exiting AR Mode

There are two ways to exit AR mode and transition to power saving mode:

- **Plug in the charging cable**

  When the start of charging is detected, the device transitions from AR mode to power saving mode.

- **Press and hold the side button for 5 seconds or more**

  The LCD display transitions to power saving mode.

  If AR space recognition fails repeatedly, using this function to exit AR mode once and returning to AR mode can make recognition easier.


## When the AR Space Is Not Recognized

When transitioning to AR mode, blocking the front of the camera may cause the AR space to fail to be recognized or make recognition difficult.


## Keeping the Camera on Standby in AR Mode

Please keep the camera on standby while capturing 3D models of the AR space within the frame.

If left in a state where 3D models in the AR space are not within the frame, or with the front of the camera blocked, recognition of the AR space may be lost. In such cases, the camera will automatically transition to power saving mode.
