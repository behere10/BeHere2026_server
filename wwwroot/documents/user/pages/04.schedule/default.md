---
title: Time Schedule
---


We recommend dividing the 6 cameras into two groups of 3 cameras each, rotating them every 3 hours so that one group is rented out and operated in AR mode while the other group is being charged.

* **11:00-14:00**: Group A
* **14:00-17:00**: Group B
* **17:00-20:00 (Friday)**: Group A
* **20:00-21:00 (Friday)**: Group B

Please keep the group that is not in AR mode connected to a charger.

Leaving devices charging overnight is also acceptable. When the battery reaches full charge, it enters an extremely low-current state, so leaving the charging cable plugged in will not cause issues.

While 4 to 5 hours of AR mode operation is theoretically possible, allowing the built-in camera battery to deplete completely can lead to lost functionality and may require manual intervention on the internal iPhone. Therefore, ensure that the camera's built-in battery is never fully depleted during operation.
