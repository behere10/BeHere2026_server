---
title: Android Tablet
---


An Android tablet is provided to monitor the camera status in more detail than status LEDs.

## Warning Displays

* **AR tracking is not available.**

  Displayed when the AR space is not recognized. This is resolved once the AR space is recognized.

* **Charging is recommended.**

  Displayed when operation in AR mode exceeds 3 hours. This is resolved when fully charged.

* **Move camera.**

  Displayed when it is estimated that the camera has been stationary for about 30 minutes, as leaving the camera still for an extended period can degrade or cancel AR space recognition. Moving the camera even slightly will resolve it, but walking around the exhibition area holding the camera is recommended. Shocks or vibrations to the camera may also be registered as camera movement. Please move the camera if it has been stationary for a long time, even if no warning appears.

* **Power bank is empty; please charge it.**

  Displayed when the camera's built-in battery becomes empty. Please charge it immediately. This is resolved once charging progresses to a certain point.

* **Switched to POWER-SAVING mode due to critical overheating.**

  Displayed when the internal temperature of the iPhone inside the camera gets too high. Please leave it in power-saving mode. This is resolved as the temperature cools down.
